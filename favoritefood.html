Pozole: Tradition in a Bowl

A comforting Mexican soup made with hominy, rich broth, and fresh toppings that turn any meal into a celebration.

Why people love Pozole?

Pozole is a traditional Mexican soup made with hominy (nixtamalized corn) simmered in a flavorful broth and served with a variety of fresh, crunchy toppings such as lettuce or cabbage, radishes, onion, lime, and oregano. It is a dish deeply connected to family gatherings, celebrations, and shared meals.

  • Hominy is the heart of the dish: warm, soft, and comforting
  • Toppings add contrast: crunchy, fresh, and acidic
  • Every region — and every family — has its own version
